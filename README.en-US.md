> Turning the chaos of urban data into smart, safe, and sustainable mobility.

**Movia** is an urban mobility assistant via WhatsApp that uses Artificial Intelligence to turn the chaos of urban data into simple decisions, offering safer, more efficient, and more sustainable routes.

**Project Status:** Currently in functional prototype (MVP) phase, validated and awarded in innovation competitions.

## The Problem

To get around a city like Rio de Janeiro, an average citizen needs to check multiple apps: one for maps, another for bus schedules, a news portal for traffic updates, and social media for security alerts. The result is an information overload that generates three daily crises:
* **Inefficiency:** Time wasted in traffic jams and with unpredictable transportation.
* **Insecurity:** Lack of consolidated data on the safety of a route.
* **Unsustainability:** Unawareness of the environmental impact of each transport choice.

## Our Solution

Movia tackles the root of the problem: information fragmentation. Instead of yet another app, we created an **intelligent conversation on WhatsApp**. The user simply asks what they need in natural language, and Movia analyzes data from multiple sources to deliver a clear, actionable insight — not just raw data.

### Key Features

* **Smart Route Consolidation:** Receive the best route in a single response that analyzes time, safety, and carbon impact.
* **Immediate Accessibility via WhatsApp:** Zero friction, no need to download another app, ensuring maximum inclusion and low data usage.
* **Community and Predictive Intelligence:** An AI that learns from anonymous community feedback to become more accurate and safer with each use.

## Tech Stack

* **Backend:** Python, Flask
* **Artificial Intelligence:** Google Gemini API
* **Database:** Google Cloud Firestore (NoSQL)
* **Geolocation:** Google Maps Directions API
* **Communication:** Twilio WhatsApp API
* **Deploy:** Gunicorn, Render.com

## Our Story and Recognition

Movia was born at the **4th Mobility Hackathon 2025**, an initiative by **Rede Mob 4.0 (COPPE/UFRJ)** and **FAPERJ**, where we had the honor of being one of the awarded teams, winning **2nd place**. This recognition validated our vision and propelled us to turn the prototype into an impact startup, which now also competes in the Desafio Liga Jovem.
